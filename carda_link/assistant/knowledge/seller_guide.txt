SELLER REGISTRATION
To participate in CardoLink as a seller, you must first register for a seller account.
Steps:
1. Open the CardoLink homepage.
2. Click the "Register" button.
3. Select "Seller" as your account type.
4. Enter your personal information, including:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Mobile Number
   - Address
   - Password
5. Upload the required identification documents if requested.
6. Read and accept the terms and conditions.
7. Click the "Register" button.
8. After successful registration, you can log in using your email and password.

------------------------------------------------------------------

REQUEST AUCTION TOKEN
Every seller must obtain an auction token before participating in an auction.
Steps:
1. Login to your CardoLink seller account.
2. Open the Seller Dashboard.
3. Click "Request Auction Token".
4. Enter the lot details:
   - Cardamom Variety
   - Estimated Weight
   - Grade
   - Harvest Date
5. Upload the required quality certificate if applicable.
6. Click "Submit".
7. The request will be sent to the administrator for verification.
8. After approval, an auction token number will be generated and displayed in your dashboard.

------------------------------------------------------------------

UPLOAD CARDAMOM LOT
Once your auction token is approved, you can upload your cardamom lot.
Steps:
1. Login to CardoLink.
2. Go to Seller Dashboard.
3. Click "Add New Lot".
4. Enter the following information:
   - Auction Token Number
   - Lot Name
   - Cardamom Variety
   - Weight (kg)
   - Grade
   - Expected Price
5. Upload clear images of the cardamom.
6. Add an optional description about the product quality.
7. Click "Submit".
8. Your lot will be available for admin verification before it appears in the auction.

------------------------------------------------------------------

TRACK LOT STATUS
You can monitor the status of every cardamom lot you upload.
Steps:
1. Login to CardoLink.
2. Open Seller Dashboard.
3. Click "My Lots".
4. Select a lot to view its details.
5. You can check:
   - Pending Approval
   - Approved
   - Live Auction
   - Sold
   - Unsold
6. You can also view:
   - Current Highest Bid
   - Number of Bids
   - Auction End Time
   - Winning Buyer (after auction completion)

------------------------------------------------------------------

VIEW AUCTION RESULTS
After the auction ends, sellers can view the auction results.
Steps:
1. Login to CardoLink.
2. Go to Seller Dashboard.
3. Open "Auction Results".
4. Select your lot.
5. View:
   - Final Selling Price
   - Highest Bid
   - Winning Buyer
   - Auction Date
   - Payment Status

------------------------------------------------------------------

PAYMENT STATUS
After your lot is sold, you can check the payment details.
Steps:
1. Login to CardoLink.
2. Open Seller Dashboard.
3. Click "Payments".
4. View:
   - Payment Amount
   - Payment Date
   - Transaction ID
   - Payment Status
5. Download the payment receipt if required.
