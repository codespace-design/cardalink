How do I become a buyer?
Where can I register as a buyer?
I want to register as buyer.
Buyer registration process.

Description:
To participate in CardaLink auctions, you must first register as a buyer account.

Steps:
1. Click the Register button on the homepage.
2. Select Buyer as the account type.
3. Enter your personal details.
4. Verify the information.
5. Submit the registration form.
6. Login using your registered email and password.
