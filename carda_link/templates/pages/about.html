What is CardaLink?

CardaLink is a comprehensive digital platform purpose-built for the cardamom industry in India — primarily serving the Idukki district of Kerala and the Western Ghats region, which account for over 80% of India's total cardamom production.

Our platform bridges the gap between cardamom growers (sellers), certified auctioneers and spice traders (buyers), and quality assurance bodies — creating a transparent, traceable, and efficient trading ecosystem.

The Cardamom Belt

Cardamom cultivation in India is concentrated in the high-altitude forests of Kerala, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu — the "Cardamom Hills." The primary trading hub is the Cardamom Trading Corporation (CTC) auction centre in Bodinayakanur, Tamil Nadu, and the Spices Board of India facilities in Idukki, Kerala.

How CardaLink Works

1. Growers Register

Cardamom farmers register their plantations, log harvest batches, and obtain Spices Board grade certifications (AGEB, AGB, AGS) directly through the platform.

2. Live Auctions

Certified auctioneers and buyers participate in real-time electronic auctions. Bids are recorded transparently with full lot traceability back to the source estate.

3. Invoicing & Traceability

Winning buyers receive digital invoices with complete lot provenance — estate, harvest date, grade, and weight — ensuring end-to-end traceability for export compliance.
